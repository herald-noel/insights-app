# Insights 💡

Insights is a modern blog platform designed for thinkers, creators, and storytellers. Whether you're a seasoned expert or just starting your journey, Insights provides a streamlined space to write, publish, and engage with a global community of like-minded individuals.

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Seamless Writing Experience:** A clean, distraction-free interface for drafting your stories.
- **Knowledge Sharing:** Categorize your posts to help others find your expertise.
- **Community Interaction:** Connect with other writers through comments and shared insights.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for reading and writing on any device, from desktop to mobile.
- **Lightning Fast:** Powered by Vite for a near-instantaneous development and user experience.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Frontend Framework:** [React](https://reactjs.org/)
- **Build Tool:** [Vite](https://vitejs.dev/)
- **UI Library:** [MUI (Material UI)](https://mui.com/)
- **Icons:** [MUI Icons](https://mui.com/material-ui/material-icons/)
- **Routing:** [React Router](https://reactrouter.com/)
- **State Management:** [Redux Toolkit](https://redux-toolkit.js.org/)
